      What to study

      • Character is chosen, not given. The same $9.00 produced ordinary-with-withholding (Sofia), ordinary-without-withholding (Maya's same-day ISO sale), and capital-gain-heading-to-zero (Ben) — fixed by grant type, elections, and clocks set long before liquidity.
      • The five-line AMT worksheet, cold. Add-back → AMTI → exemption with the phaseout → 26%/28% tentative → excess over regular tax. Know the ~42% shadow bracket in the phaseout band, the credit, and the same-year-sale escape.
      • Two clocks start at exercise — none while you wait. The one-year capital-gains clock and the QSBS holding period both run from exercise, and the QSBS asset test is applied at exercise too.
      • Withholding is not the tax. 29.35% withheld against a 42.35% true rate; ISO events withhold nothing at all; phantom income on an illiquid exercise has to be funded with real cash.
      • The deductibility asymmetry. NQSO spreads and same-day ISO sales are deductible compensation to the company; qualifying ISO gains and long-held shares give it nothing.
      • Plan design is tax policy. 90-day windows, the three-month ISO statute, early-exercise availability, and 409A discipline after a secondary print are CFO levers — each with a named payer. Forfeiture of unvested awards reverses recognized expense; vested expiry reverses nothing; extensions are modifications.
      • The final exam will not retest Solstice's facts. It will hand you a new grant history and a liquidity event and expect the machinery above run on it — the decision tree with dollars attached.
